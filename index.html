01 / CURIOUS

It started with curiosity.

I wanted to know what happened behind the screen, beneath the interface and inside the systems I was using.

02 / BUILDER

Then curiosity became something I could build.

Small experiments became programs, games and ideas that existed because I decided to make them real.

03 / EXPERIMENTER

I started building simply to see what would happen.

Some things worked. Most things changed. The useful part was always the iteration in between.

04 / THINKER

Eventually, building wasn't enough.

I wanted to understand why things worked, where they failed and how the pieces connected.

05 / NOW

I build. I experiment. I learn. I keep going.

That is the thread running through the work that follows.
